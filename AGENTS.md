Company / hackathon bar: a **focused, working product** with evidence — not demo slop or unfinished ambition.

- `.cursor/rules/` — engineering, security, secrets, frontend craft, **hackathon-judging-rubric**
- Skills: `company-production-gate`, `secrets-hygiene`, `frontend-craft`, `hackathon-judging`

## Judging (100 pts) — optimize in this spirit

| Area | Pts | Win condition |
|------|-----|----------------|
| Problem Understanding & Relevance | 15 | Clear users + verification problem fit |
| Data Quality & Signal | 10 | Clean data paths; verified scores drive matching |
| Software Engineering & Execution | 25 | Working prototype, solid architecture, great UX |
| Validation, Testing & Evidence | 15 | Tests, demos, metrics, named limitations |
| Proof of Demand & Impact | 10 | Real hiring need; process improvement story |
| Relevance & Readiness to Deploy | 15 | Usable now; deployable; reliable main path |
| Presentation & Transparency | 10 | Clear pitch/demo; disclose AI + limits |

Principles: evidence-based · MVP over vapor · ML optional · responsible AI.  
Tie-break: more event tools → readiness → validation → SE → demand → real-world value.

## Secrets

Never expose keys, JWT secrets, DB passwords, tokens, or raw `.env`. Confirm set/missing only; redact; rotate on leak.

## Frontend

No AI-slop UI. No Vite scaffold as product. Use `frontend_design_bar` / `frontend_audit` / `frontend-craft`.

## Expectation

Prioritize with `judging_scorecard`. Close the gaps that cost the most points. Ship seeker + employer main paths that judges can run without the team.
