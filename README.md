# ThibitishaSkillz

## Problem Statement

The recruitment process is increasingly affected by a disconnect between what candidates claim on their CVs and what they can actually demonstrate in terms of knowledge, skills and experience. Employers often rely heavily on CVs, qualifications and interviews to evaluate applicants, yet these methods do not always establish whether candidates genuinely possess the competencies required for a role, particularly in an era where AI can generate polished CVs, applications and interview responses.


Additionally, employers face challenges in screening large volumes of applications, identifying suitable candidates, conducting consistent assessments and communicating recruitment outcomes efficiently.


Our proposed solution is an AI-powered recruitment and competency verification platform that integrates job-board listings, CV analysis, personalised clarification questions and interactive AI conversations to assess candidates against specific job requirements. The platform evaluates candidates' responses against the skills and experience claimed in their CVs, identifies competency gaps and ranks applicants based on evidence of their suitability. It then generates a shortlist of the top 10 candidates and prospective top 10 candidates, while facilitating automated SMS and email notifications to shortlisted applicants for final interviews.
The ultimate goal is to help employers move beyond CV-based recruitment towards a more efficient, transparent and evidence-based hiring process that prioritises demonstrated competence over self-reported qualifications.

## The core problem our product addresses

How might we help employers identify and shortlist genuinely qualified candidates by verifying their actual competencies against job requirements, rather than relying solely on CVs, AI-generated applications and conventional interviews?

## Key problems being solved

1.	CV credibility: Difficulty establishing whether candidates genuinely possess the skills and experience stated in their CVs.
2.	Inefficient screening: Time-consuming manual review of large volumes of applications.
3.	AI dependency: Difficulty distinguishing genuine candidate understanding from AI-generated responses.
4.	Inconsistent assessments: Lack of standardised, role-specific evaluation of candidates.
5.	Poor candidate matching: Difficulty identifying applicants whose demonstrated competencies align with job requirements.
6.	Communication delays: Inefficient communication of interview invitations and recruitment updates.
